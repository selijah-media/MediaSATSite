Narrative Structure

The thematic concepts behind my photographs will revolve around the development of self. I am to present the complexities of an internal state and the duality between things we wish we had and the things we do not. In a projector photography photoshoot, the conceptual background lies in the fusion of two distinct forms of art: photography and projection. By incorporating a projected image into a photo, I can create visually striking and thought-provoking compositions that blur the lines between reality and imagination. The projected image serves as a dynamic element that adds depth, texture, and narrative to the scene, transforming a static photograph into a multi-dimensional visual experience. This technique allows me to employ light, shadow, colour, and scale in innovative ways, opening up a world of creative possibilities.

The incorporation of a projected image in a photo can symbolise various concepts. It can represent the juxtaposition of past and present, the merging of different perspectives, or the exploration of personal memories and emotions. By integrating a projected image into my work, I can convey complex ideas, evoke emotions, and engage viewers in a dialogue that goes beyond the surface of the photograph. Overall, projector photography offers a unique and captivating approach to image-making that challenges traditional notions of photography and expands the boundaries of visual storytelling.


My series of projector photography photoshoots examine emotions, loss, and desire, with a focus on adults aged 25 to 45 who are going through life changes or looking for greater significance. It is aimed at individuals interested in art, psychology, and self-exploration, regardless of gender. The audience is likely to have a higher education, which allows for involvement with challenging topics. The series is intended to appeal to anyone who enjoy creatively exploring human emotions. It also appeals to those who value authenticity and sensitivity in art, as it connects with shared human experiences. Subsets of the target age group could include young professionals, parents, or those going through personal transitions. The text fosters contemplating on the past, present, and future, promoting self-contemplation. Overall, the series brings together varied backgrounds on a voyage of self-discovery.

This picture demonstrates effective simplicity in narrative structure. Regarding the subject, their age, their experiences, and the causes of the feelings they are feeling, not much is known. Because there is no context, the viewer is free to draw from their own experiences, which deepens the emotional impact of the image. I want to use my own photos to explore this idea: the story should be straightforward but powerful. Storytelling elements through body language, facial expression, composition, shot size, and colour will play a crucial role in the development of meaning.

The atmosphere is gently lit, with soft ambient lighting creating subtle shadows throughout the space. The person stands in the centre, lighted by a flickering glow from a projector pointed straight at them. The projector, placed just behind the photographer, casts a stunning image of crackling flames onto the subject's exposed chest and shoulders. Furthermore, the person, a symbol of raw vulnerability, stands shirtless in front of the projector. Their posture is relaxed but grounded, projecting a sense of peaceful strength. Their facial expression is natural and free of forced emotion, allowing the viewer to explore the depths of their inner world. The choice to show fire contains considerable symbolism. Fire has traditionally been connected with transformation, renewal, and cleansing. It consumes the old to make room for the new, representing the process of shedding old burdens and embracing personal growth. In this setting, the projected flames symbolize the subject's inner struggle and the cathartic process of self-discovery.

The lack of clothing enhances vulnerability and reveals the subject's actual nature. They are stripped of their social conceptions and material trappings, allowing the observer to reflect and empathise.

NARRATIVE: EVALUATION

I aim to depict the thematic and narrative elements through a series of 5 photographs. As my final shots are developed, there will be a projected secondary image in every picture. This choice will serve the purpose of enhancing my display's visual dynamics to draw in viewers. In my opinion, the addition of a secondary photo will contribute to framing the subject in a direct manner and reveal the inherent emotion that is necessary to give meaning and narrative. Parts of the story are created through the duality between the subject, the environment, and the projected image. Through codes such as lighting, contrast, camera angle, and frame within a frame, a narrative will be constructed within each image. There will not be an overarching story to be told; only individual stores, as depicted through my themes which will form genre through similar visual dynamics. In terms of audience engagement and consumption of my photographs: I feel as though having five highly potent images will serve my themes better as opposed to having 10 separate images, which might convolute the narrative being told and how impactful the displays are. The structure of time throughout my final narrative will not be an element that is considered. Instead, I am aiming to convey narrative through thematics and not a linear process of time / order.
